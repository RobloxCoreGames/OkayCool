Jib Simulator

A camera crane with a remote head. Walk up to it, grab the handlebar and fly the shot while you watch the monitor.

Checking for VR support…

In the headset (hands or controllers)
  • Grab: close your fist around a part (or squeeze the grip button on a controller).
  • Handlebar: push, pull, lift and lower it to swing and boom the arm. It has real weight and keeps drifting when you let go.
  • Left grip: twist it like a motorbike throttle to zoom. Roll it back to zoom in, forward to zoom out.
  • Joystick (right side of the control box): pans and tilts the camera head.
  • Focus knob (left side of the box): turn it to pull focus, or press AF to make it follow the actor.
  • Counterweights: grab and slide them to balance the arm.
  • Column: grab it to roll the whole dolly.
  • Buttons: poke them with a fingertip. REC, PLAY, AF, GUIDE, DRAG, FIT. FIT moves you behind the handlebar and sets the height for you.
  • Controllers also have shortcuts: right stick pans and tilts, left stick zooms and focuses, A records, B plays back.
On a computer (for testing)
  • WASD push the arm. ←→↑↓ pan and tilt.
  • Q/E zoom, Z/C focus, F AF, R record, P play, G guides, T drag.
  • Drag the mouse to look around.
  • Gamepad: left stick moves the arm, right stick pans and tilts, the triggers zoom, the bumpers focus, A records, B plays, X toggles AF, Y toggles guides.
